Earlier this year I lost my father — a teacher, social worker, and public servant, and a beloved figure in his hometown in Nepal, who devoted his life to making the world around him better. He taught me that quiet perseverance and service to others matter more than any title, and his spirit lives on through me in everything I do.

These past few years have tested me — including a long recovery from more than a year of long COVID — and the months since his passing have been the hardest of my life. I share this because I believe mental health matters as much as professional success. Through all of it I have kept going, grateful to everyone who has supported me, and I am still here — rebuilding, resilient, and looking forward to solving hard problems in safety-critical controls and robotics.

To my father: I will spend the rest of my life making you proud.

*With gratitude,*
*Sandesh*
